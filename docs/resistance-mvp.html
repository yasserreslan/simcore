Resistance MVP

The smallest game that still feels like the Resistance: one map, a mirror match against an AI, and every pillar in its simplest form. Numbers, cheap drones, light vehicles, a rocket fleet that runs dry, supply lines and tunnels are all in. Everything else waits.

01

The mapAgreed

One 96×96 map with two bases in opposite corners. Each base starts next to 2 visible gold mines; 4 more are hidden under fog, at spots the match seed picks, so scouting pays every game. Oil sits in a contested field in the middle.

In MVP

  • One hand-made 2-player map, 96×96
  • Fog of war: unexplored is dark, explored but unseen is grey
  • 2 visible gold mines per base, 4 hidden
  • One central oil field

Later

  • More maps, 2v2
  • Per-map mine rules (some fixed, some random)
  • Map editor

Decided

  • Hidden mines: the map marks possible spots, the match seed picks which hold gold
  • Size: 96×96
  • Mines: 2 visible per base + 4 hidden
02

EconomyAgreed

Start with a cash grant. Gold is fast and simple: a worker walks to a mine and carries each load back to the HQ, so far mines pay less and workers on the road can be raided. Mines slowly run dry. Oil pays more over time, but needs an expensive derrick and a refinery built within range of it, out where the enemy can reach.

In MVP

  • Starting cash
  • Gold mines worked by workers, no building needed
  • Mines slowly run dry
  • Oil: derrick on an oil spot, refinery within range of it

Later

  • Hidden supply caches
  • Drop-off camps near far mines
  • Pipelines or oil trucks

Decided

  • Workers carry gold back to the HQ
  • Oil: range rule, no pipeline on the map
  • Mines run dry slowly

04

Infantry & loadoutsAgreed

Four combat classes plus the worker, trained one soldier at a time. A soldier carries two things at once: a primary and a secondary. Before the match each player saves up to 3 presets per class, each one a pair of picks from that class's list. Skills like medic, spotter and heavy anti-tank come with later archetypes.

AssaultLight frontline. Primary: rifle, SMG or LMG.
EngineerUtility. Kit: booby traps, FPV drones or repair.
Anti-armorKills vehicles and drones. E.g. AT missile + booby trap, or AA missile + short-range rifle.
SniperLong range, sees far. Sniper rifle + short-range gun.
WorkerBuilds, mines gold. No loadout.

In MVP

  • 4 classes + worker, trained one at a time
  • Two slots per soldier: primary + secondary
  • Up to 3 presets per class, saved before the match
  • Any class can drive a vehicle

Later

  • Skills from later archetypes: medic, spotter, heavy AT
  • Engineer role against armor
  • Perks and a points budget per preset

Decided

  • Single soldiers, not squads
  • Assault primary: rifle, SMG or LMG
  • Sniper: sniper rifle + short-range gun
  • Anti-armor pairs, e.g. AT missile + booby trap, AA missile + short-range rifle
  • Engineer has no anti-armor role for now
  • Secondary choices for Assault and Engineer
05

Vehicles & crewAgreed

Vehicles roll out of the yard empty. Any of your infantry can get in and drive, and every driver is equal; without a driver a vehicle can't move or fire. If it's destroyed, the driver has a chance to bail out.

In MVP

  • Six vehicles, one seat each
  • Enter and exit your own vehicles
  • Driver may survive when the vehicle is destroyed

Later

  • Gunner seats and passengers
  • Crew class bonuses (e.g. an engineer driver repairs)
  • Capturing empty enemy vehicles

Decided

  • Driver survival is a chance, drawn from the match seed
  • No capturing enemy vehicles
  • Any driver is equal
06

Rocket fleet & supplyAgreed

Launchers fire salvos that scatter, and they carry only a few. Empty launchers go quiet until an ammo truck or a supply depot refills them. Ammo trucks carry a limited stock and restock at a depot, so the supply line runs from base to front and protecting it is part of attacking. Units near a supply source reload faster.

In MVP

  • Light, medium and heavy launchers
  • Limited salvos, scatter, rockets in flight (can miss moving targets)
  • Rearm from ammo truck or depot
  • Ammo trucks restock at a depot
  • Supply radius: faster reload
  • Launchers need someone to see the target; the heavy one needs a spotter far ahead

Later

  • Ammo through the tunnel network
  • More launcher types

Decided

  • Ammo trucks have limited stock, restock at a depot
  • Supply radius gives faster reload
  • Salvos and rockets per launcher: tuned in playtests
07

DronesAgreed

Drones are an engineer kit, not a unit you build. An engineer with the drone kit carries 2 cheap FPV drones. You pick a target and the drone flies there and explodes on contact; while it's in the air it can be shot down. Spent drones restock from an ammo truck or depot.

In MVP

  • FPV kamikaze drone as an engineer kit, 2 per engineer
  • Pick a unit or spot; the drone flies there on its own
  • Can be shot down in flight
  • Restock at ammo truck or depot

Later

  • Scout drone
  • Steering drones by hand
  • Drone launcher vehicle or truck that carries larger drones

Decided

  • Pick a target, the drone flies itself
  • Hard counter: the anti-armor AA missile; rifles and MGs hit drones only weakly
  • 2 drones per engineer
08

TunnelsAgreed

Tunnel entrances form a network. Each entrance links to every other entrance within about 15 cells, and units travel along the links (fast, not instant). There is no root: any linked group works on its own. The risk: destroy a central entrance and any entrance left with no link at all collapses instantly. Units travelling through it pop out, damaged, at the nearest working entrance.

In MVP

  • Tunnel entrances built by workers
  • Links to every entrance within ~15 cells
  • Travel time based on link distance
  • Isolated entrances collapse instantly

Later

  • Ammo and supply flowing through tunnels
  • Tunnel upgrades (faster, longer links)

Decided

  • No root: any linked group of entrances works
  • An entrance with no links collapses instantly
  • Units in transit pop out, damaged, at the nearest working entrance
  • Link range ~15 cells
09

Combat & trapsAgreed

Every weapon deals a warhead type, every target has an armor class, and a table decides how much gets through. Engineers with the trap kit, and anti-armor soldiers with a trap secondary, plant hidden booby traps that fire when an enemy ground unit steps close. Enemy snipers and engineers reveal traps near them; once revealed, any unit can shoot them.

In MVP

  • Damage table above, with a drone class
  • Booby traps: hidden, one use
  • Snipers and engineers reveal nearby traps
  • Splash for rockets and explosives

Later

  • Heavy armor (for the second faction)
  • Veterancy
  • Infantry cover and garrisoning buildings

Decided

  • Snipers and engineers detect traps
  • Drones are their own armor class
  • Traps per soldier: tuned in playtests
10

Test AI & winningAgreed

The MVP AI is a tuning tool, not a fun opponent. Scripted bots play the same Resistance with fixed strategies, so we can pit them against each other in hundreds of headless matches and read off win rates and match lengths, and so you have something to play against while tuning. You win by destroying every enemy structure and defense.

In MVP

  • Scripted test bots with a few fixed strategies (e.g. infantry rush, launcher push, tunnel raids)
  • Headless batch runs: win rate and match length per strategy and per preset
  • You can play a full match against a bot
  • Win: every enemy structure and defense destroyed

Later

  • LAN 1v1: the next milestone right after the MVP
  • A real opponent AI with difficulty levels
  • Online 1v1

Decided

  • Vs AI first, LAN right after
  • AI is for testing and tuning, not for fun yet
  • Starting defenses count toward the win

Build order to MVPAgreed

Units first. Phase 1 gets you looking at real units and playing a battle against a simple, tunable bot before any base or economy exists. Each milestone ends in something you can watch or play.

Phase 1 · Units you can see and play

Two armies spawn on the map; no base, no economy. Win by wiping out the other army.

  1. Content model for units. Partly built Classes, two-slot loadouts, vehicles and kits as data; redo the sample content for this roster.Done when: the Resistance units run from data files and the golden replay passes on every OS.
  2. Art pipeline + Godot shell. The Assault soldier modeled in Blender, animated and rendered in 8 directions, drawn by Godot from the sim.Done when: you watch soldiers walk and shoot with real sprites.
  3. Controls and group movement. Select, move, attack, control groups; units stop overlapping.Done when: you can command a squad through a gap without jamming.
  4. The full unit roster. Four classes and their loadouts, vehicles with crew, light, medium and heavy launchers, ammo trucks, drones, traps.Done when: every unit in this page works in a battle.
  5. Tunable test bot. A simple bot that fights you, plus headless bot-vs-bot battles with every tuning value in data files.Done when: you play a battle against it, change a number, and see the result change.

Phase 2 · Base and economy

  1. Structures and workers. The six structures, training queues, starting defenses, win condition.Done when: you build a full base from the HQ and win by razing the enemy's.
  2. Gold and oil. Mining, carrying, depletion, derricks and refineries, supply radius.Done when: a match runs on its own economy.

Phase 3 · Map systems

  1. Fog of war. Vision, hidden mines, trap detection.Done when: a hidden mine appears only after a unit sees it.
  2. Tunnels. Network, travel time, collapse.Done when: killing a central entrance collapses the isolated ones.
  3. Balance runs. Test bots with fixed strategies, 100-match batches.Done when: win rates per strategy are reported. This is the MVP; LAN 1v1 comes next.

Not in the MVPAgreed

Kept out on purpose. Each one fits the engine later as new data or one new behavior.

  • Second faction with planes and heavy armor
  • Scout drones and drone vehicles
  • Medic, spotter and heavy AT skills
  • Hidden supply caches
  • Defensive structures and upgrades
  • Gunner seats, passengers, vehicle capture
  • Ammo through tunnels
  • LAN 1v1 (first milestone after the MVP)
  • Opponent AI with difficulty levels
  • Online multiplayer and matchmaking
  • Campaign and story
  • More maps, 2v2, map editor
  • Scavenging (dropped)
Setting: real-world, present day, with fictional countries, groups and leaders. Resistance only for now; the mirror match keeps balance simple while the systems settle.
