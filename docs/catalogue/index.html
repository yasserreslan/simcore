The Resistance
Unit Catalogue

Every soldier, truck and launcher the Resistance can field in a personal real-time strategy game, with the real numbers from the engine data, the preview renders from the art pipeline and a briefing for each file.

The Resistance is a fictional faction in a fictional setting. Gear is described generically; no real organisation, flag, insignia or text is depicted. Stats are read from the game's content files and are starting values that will change. All images are renders of the game's own models.

HOW TO READ A FILE: ranges and speeds are converted from the engine's tenths of a cell (1 cell = 2 m); reloads from ticks (20 per second). Stat bars use a fixed scale across the whole catalogue (HP 300, speed 9 m/s, range and damage square-root scaled to 200 m and 550). Damage is per hit before armour; the armour row shows the direct hit against each class.

SECTION 01

Infantry

Six classes of soldier, one body and rig. Cheap, quick and fragile: they win by numbers, loadouts and the right tool for the job.

RS-INF-01

Assault

Infantry

Line infantry. Rifle, SMG or LMG plus a sidearm.

Every campaign starts with someone willing to hold a doorway. The assault soldier carries whatever the section needs: a rifle for reach, an SMG for corners, a belt-fed LMG for the long argument. He is cheap enough that the Resistance fields him in numbers nobody can count.
